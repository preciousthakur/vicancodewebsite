1. Introduction & Scope

Vican Code Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and integrity of personal and enterprise information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy governs our privacy practices across our corporate website (www.vicancode.com) and our proprietary software-as-a-service platforms, applications, and services.

This document has been crafted in strict compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules), the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India, and global data privacy regulations where applicable (including GDPR).

2. Product-Specific Data Practices

Vican Code Private Limited engineers and operates multiple flagship software applications. Each platform operates with specific privacy parameters:

A. DGate (dgate.in) — Society OS & Visitor Management

DGate processes society resident directories, gate pass requests, guard walk-in logs, and maintenance dues. Visitor Photo Privacy: Photos captured at security gates are stored on secured, private object storage and accessed strictly via time-limited, expiring signed URLs. They are never published on open directories or indexed by web search engines. Data is retained strictly according to RWA audit guidelines and Indian local regulations.

B. Educan (educan.io) — Cloud School Management Software

Educan processes student attendance, guardian contact records, exam marks, and fee payment statuses. Student records are classified as sensitive and accessible solely by verified school administrators, teachers, and enrolled parents through role-based access control.

C. VicanTools (vicantools.com) — 100% Private Client-Side Browser Utilities

Zero File Upload Architecture: VicanTools utilities (PDF Merger, PDF Compressor, Image Converter, and developer tools) run 100% locally within your browser using WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas. Your files, media, and documents never leave your computer or touch our servers.

D. PracEasy (praceasy.in) — CA Practice Management SaaS

PracEasy provides multi-tenant practice management for Indian Chartered Accountancy firms. All client compliances, GSTIN records, and client portal documents are encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. All server infrastructure is hosted within Indian sovereign data centers.

E. VicanThemes (vicanthemes.com) — Digital Atelier & Marketplace

Processes buyer account profiles, billing details, and theme download tokens. Payment processing is managed via authorized PCI-DSS compliant third-party gateways (such as Razorpay and Stripe); full payment card data is never stored on our servers.

F. MyBankIFSCCode (mybankifsccode.com) — Bank Directory Platform

An informational public bank directory and loan calculation suite. No personal banking credentials or financial transactions are captured or requested.
