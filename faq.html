How can we help?

What is Vican Code Private Limited?

Vican Code Private Limited is an incorporated Indian technology enterprise (CIN: U62012PB2026PTC069843) registered in Derabassi, Punjab. We provide end-to-end custom software engineering, mobile app development (iOS & Android), cloud infrastructure, and operate our own proprietary SaaS product ecosystem.

What are your flagship SaaS products?

We build and scale 6 major platforms: DGate (residential society OS & visitor management), Educan (cloud school ERP), VicanTools (100% private browser utility suite), VicanThemes (design atelier for HTML & React kits), PracEasy (practice SaaS for Indian CA firms), and MyBankIFSCCode (banking lookup & loan calculators).

How does DGate help housing societies & RWAs?

DGate modernizes gated communities by eliminating manual paper registers. It offers instant resident approvals via push notification, QR gate passes, photo-backed guard entry logs, SOS alerts, and automated maintenance collections with Razorpay integration — all in one simple per-flat subscription.

What technologies and frameworks do you specialize in?

Our core engineering stack comprises modern technologies including Next.js, React, Vue 3, Node.js, Python, Swift, Kotlin, Flutter, React Native, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, and AWS/GCP cloud architectures.

How does VicanTools protect user privacy?

VicanTools is engineered with a strict zero-upload architecture. Utilities like the PDF Merger, Image Compressor, and Page Remover execute 100% locally in your browser using WebAssembly. Your files never touch our servers, guaranteeing complete confidentiality.

What is PracEasy and who is it designed for?

PracEasy is a dedicated practice management SaaS platform for Indian Chartered Accountant (CA) firms and tax professionals. It tracks all filing deadlines (GST, ITR, Audit, ROC, TDS) with a dual-portal setup: an internal portal for firm staff and a self-service upload portal for clients.

Where is customer and platform data hosted?

All sensitive customer and SaaS database instances are hosted on sovereign cloud servers located within India. We enforce 256-bit encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest, maintaining strict compliance with the Indian DPDP Act 2023.
